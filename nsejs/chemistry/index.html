NSEJS-2024-C-Q55 {Unvalidated} Demand: SYNTHESIZE · Band D3
Format: Multiple Correct (6 Marks)
Consider the phosphorus oxyacids: hypophosphorous acid ($H_3PO_2$), phosphorous acid ($H_3PO_3$), and orthophosphoric acid ($H_3PO_4$). Which of the following statements are correct?
Commit answer to unlock official derivation.
Official Solution & Protected Crux Move [NSEJS-2024-C-Q55-M2] {Unvalidated}:
1. Structure & Basicity: In phosphorus oxyacids, only hydrogens bound to oxygen (P-OH) are ionizable. $H_3PO_2$ has one P-OH (monobasic); $H_3PO_3$ has two P-OH (dibasic); $H_3PO_4$ has three P-OH (tribasic). Basicity: $1 < 2 < 3$. (A and C are true).
2. Reducing Property: Reducing character is directly proportional to number of P-H bonds. $H_3PO_2$ has two P-H bonds; $H_3PO_4$ has zero P-H bonds. (B is true).
3. Oxidation States: $+1$ in $H_3PO_2$, $+3$ in $H_3PO_3$, $+5$ in $H_3PO_4$. (D is false). Correct Options: A, B, C.
