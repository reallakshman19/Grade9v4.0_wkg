Format: Single Correct (3 Marks)
An ant is sitting on the principal axis of a convex mirror of focal length $f$, at a distance $2f$ from the pole in front of the mirror. It starts moving on the principal axis towards the mirror. During the course of motion, the distance between the ant and its image:
Sample only — use the topic bank for a saved local first attempt.
Candidate explanation & proposed reasoning [NSEJS-2023-P-Q01-M2] {Unvalidated}:
1. Physical Model: For a convex mirror, $f > 0$. When ant is at $u = -2f$, mirror equation $1/v + 1/(-2f) = 1/f$ gives $v = +2f/3$. Separation $D(u) = |u| + v = 2f + 2f/3 = 8f/3$.
2. Dynamic Execution: As ant moves towards pole ($u \to 0^-$), $v = \frac{f|u|}{|u|+f}$. Total separation is $D(|u|) = |u| + \frac{f|u|}{|u|+f}$. Its derivative $dD/d|u| = 1 + \frac{f^2}{(|u|+f)^2} > 0$ strictly positive for all $|u| \ge 0$.
3. Resolution: Separation $D$ continuously decreases as $|u|$ decreases from $2f \to 0$. Correct Option: C.
NSEJS-2024-P-Q15 {Unvalidated} Demand: SYNTHESIZE · Band D3
Format: Multiple Correct (6 Marks)
A monochromatic light ray passes symmetrically through an equilateral glass prism of refractive index $\mu = \sqrt{3}$. Which of the following statements are correct?
Sample only — use the topic bank for a saved local first attempt.
Candidate explanation & proposed reasoning [NSEJS-2024-P-Q15-M2] {Unvalidated}:
1. Model: Equilateral prism $A = 60^\circ$. At minimum deviation, $r_1 = r_2 = A/2 = 30^\circ$.
2. Snell's Law: $\sin i_1 = \mu \sin r_1 = \sqrt{3} \sin 30^\circ = \sqrt{3}/2 \implies i_1 = 60^\circ$. (B is true).
3. Deviation: $\delta_m = 2i - A = 2(60^\circ) - 60^\circ = 60^\circ$. (A is true). By symmetry, internal ray is parallel to base (C is true). Correct Options: A, B, C.
