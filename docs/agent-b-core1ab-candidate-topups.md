## Physics — coalescence (provisional target: PHYS-NSEJS-FLUID-04)

**Core1A top-up:** A fluid volume sum uses the cube of each starting radius; total interfacial area uses the square. The usual N-identical-droplet equation **cannot** simply be applied to droplets of unequal radii. State assumptions: fixed liquid volume, constant positive surface tension, spherical isolated interfaces, no evaporation, no surfactant and ignoring changes in bulk/thermal free energy. The reduction in surface-area contribution to free energy does not, alone, prove immediate spontaneous merger under every material and kinetic condition.

**Fresh Core1B W (unequal radii):** Eight water drops of radius 1 mm and one water drop of radius 2 mm merge into one spherical drop at constant volume. With surface tension 0.072 N/m, derive total eliminated area and released surface free-energy contribution.

**Independent result:** R³=(8+2³)r³=16r³; A_i=4πr²(8+2²)=4πr²×12; A_f=4πr²×16^(2/3); ΔA=7.100496764×10^-5 m²; γΔA=5.11235767×10^-6 J. Fraction eliminated ≈47.0866%.

**Plausible false model:** Treat the large drop as one of nine identical drops, yielding R=9^(1/3)r. Falsifier: incorrect volume 9r³ versus actual 16r³.

**Maximum H3:** Ask for the sum of cubic radii; do not supply R, final area or energy.
