Official examination scope

The Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT) describes NSEJS 2026 as covering Physics, Chemistry and Biology through Class 10. Grade9 currently has a Physics pilot and planned Mathematics/Chemistry subject workspaces, but no Biology workspace. Therefore this is Physics Foundation / NSEJS-aligned topic guidance only, not complete NSEJS preparation.
